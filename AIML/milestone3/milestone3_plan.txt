Milestone 3 - AI/ML Upgrade Plan

1. Sales Forecasting
- Previous Model: Basic Forecasting Model
- New Model: XGBoost or Random Forest
- Improvement Goal:
    - Lower MAE
    - Lower RMSE
    - Better prediction accuracy

2. Customer Segmentation
- Previous Approach:
    - K-Means Clustering
- Improvement:
    - Better feature selection
    - Improved customer grouping

3. Churn Prediction
- Previous Approach:
    - Purchase frequency rules
- Improvement:
    - Reduce false positives
    - Improve risk classification

4. Product Recommendation
- Previous Approach:
    - Category-based recommendations
- Improvement:
    - Frequently bought together analysis
    - Better product suggestions

5. Anomaly Detection
- Previous Approach:
    - Simple sales threshold
- Improvement:
    - Statistical anomaly detection
    - Reduce false alarms

6. Dataset
- Same Superstore dataset from Milestones 1 and 2 will be reused.
- No new dataset will be created.
